# Fundora Capital

**The Prop Firm for Solana Memes.**

Pass the evaluation. Get funded. Trade meme coins. Withdraw your profits in **SOL**.

No KYC. No accounts. No centralized custody.

**Your wallet is your identity.**

---

## Overview

Fundora Capital is a decentralized proprietary trading platform built for the Solana ecosystem. Traders complete an evaluation, receive funded capital upon passing, and withdraw profits directly in SOL.

By leveraging wallet-based authentication, Fundora removes traditional onboarding friction while providing a seamless on-chain trading experience.

---

## Key Features

- ⚡ Wallet-based authentication
- 🚫 No KYC required
- 💰 Withdraw rewards directly in SOL
- 🎯 Proprietary trading evaluations
- 🧠 Built specifically for Solana meme traders
- 🔒 Non-custodial identity
- 📈 Transparent on-chain experience
- 🌐 Fast and low-cost Solana transactions

## How It Works

1. Connect your Solana wallet.
2. Start the trading evaluation.
3. Meet the required performance targets.
4. Receive a funded trading account.
5. Trade eligible Solana meme assets.
6. Withdraw your profits in SOL.
